Experiment metrics summary
==========================

Evaluation split: validation set (400 samples)
Value domain: log10 resistivity values stored in the MAT files
best_val_loss: minimum validation loss during training
trained epochs: actual completed epochs before early stopping (maximum configured: 100)
average_epoch_time: arithmetic mean of epoch_time_sec over all completed epochs
RMSE, MAE and R2: pooled over all validation pixels
PSNR and SSIM: mean of the 400 per-sample values

For closed-loop inversion, best_val_loss is the weighted total loss:
0.8 * L_inv + 0.2 * L_fwd. The inverse image metrics evaluate only
the final predicted resistivity models.
